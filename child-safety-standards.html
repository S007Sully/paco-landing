Paco Revolution

Child Safety Standards

Effective date: June 11, 2026

Paco Revolution is a members-only social, dating, and events application operated by Paco Revolution LLC. These Child Safety Standards describe our zero-tolerance commitment to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and the dissemination of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on the Paco Revolution app and website (the "Service").

1. Adults Only

Paco Revolution is strictly for adults 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to create an account or use the Service. Accounts found to belong to a minor are removed immediately.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAE and CSAM

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. The following are strictly prohibited on Paco Revolution and result in immediate removal and reporting to the proper authorities:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including any image, video, or text that sexualizes a minor.
  • Any content or conduct that sexualizes, exploits, endangers, or grooms a minor.
  • Solicitation, facilitation, or promotion of the sexual abuse or exploitation of a minor.
  • Any attempt to use the Service to contact, lure, or harm a minor.
  • Trafficking of any person, including minors.

3. In-App Reporting and Feedback

Paco Revolution provides an in-app mechanism for users to report content, profiles, or behavior, and to block other members. Reports of suspected CSAE or CSAM are prioritized and reviewed promptly. You can also report concerns directly to our Child Safety point of contact listed below.

4. How We Address CSAM

When we identify or receive a report of CSAM or suspected CSAE, we:

  • Remove the content and suspend or permanently ban the responsible account.
  • Preserve relevant information as required by law.
  • Report to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and to law enforcement as required by applicable law.
  • Cooperate with lawful requests from authorities investigating child safety.

5. Compliance With Laws

Paco Revolution complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including reporting obligations regarding CSAM. We maintain these standards and our reporting practices to keep the Service safe.

6. Child Safety Point of Contact

For child safety questions, concerns, or reports, contact our designated Child Safety point of contact:

Paco Revolution LLC, Child Safety Team
Email: pacorevolutionapp@gmail.com

You may also report child sexual exploitation directly to the NCMEC CyberTipline at report.cybertip.org or, in an emergency, contact your local law enforcement.

These Child Safety Standards apply to the Paco Revolution app and website operated by Paco Revolution LLC. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
